# Selling sponsor slots on the guest QR page

The restaurant pays for Jamavat. The **sponsor** pays for the guest's
attention while the food is cooking. Same scooter, same phone, different
door — and this list is the doors.

Read `SALES_SURAT.md` first: the method (one area a day, numbers off Maps
that morning, every contact into HQ → Leads) is identical. Only the pitch
and the price change.

---

## 1. What you are actually selling

A guest scans the QR, reads the menu, orders, and then sits on the
"served in a few minutes" screen for **ten to fifteen minutes** with the
phone in their hand. That is the slot.

Per restaurant, per month, roughly:

| | |
|---|---|
| Tables | 20 |
| Sittings per table per day | 3 |
| Days | 30 |
| **Guest phone sessions** | **~1,800** |

Five restaurants in one area = **~9,000 local, adult, out-of-the-house,
already-spending people a month**, all within three kilometres of the
sponsor's shop.

Anchor the price against what they already buy:

| What they spend on today | Monthly cost | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| Hoarding on Ring Road | ₹12,000–25,000 | Everyone, mostly driving past |
| One post on a local Instagram page | ₹1,500–3,000 | Once, then gone |
| Newspaper insert | ₹6,000–10,000 | Thrown with the paper |
| Pamphlet distribution | ₹4,000 | Binned at the gate |
| **Jamavat guest screen, one area** | **₹3,000** | 9,000 people, 10 minutes each, phone in hand |

That table is the whole pitch. Keep it on your phone as a photo.

## 2. Who to walk into

A sponsor is worth approaching when **all three** are true:

| | Why |
|---|---|
| **Shop is within 3 km of the restaurants** | "Aa j vistaar na loko" is the entire reason the slot is worth more than Google's |
| **Sells to walk-in consumers**, not to businesses | A guest finishing dinner can act on a salon offer, not on a packaging supplier |
| **Already spends on local advertising** | Hoarding, pamphlets, Instagram pages, cricket-ground banners. Someone who has never advertised has to be taught the habit first — skip them in round one |

### The categories, in the order they say yes

**Tier 1 — walk in first.** High ticket, local-only customers, and they
already buy hoardings.

| Category | Why they say yes | What to search on Maps |
|---|---|---|
| **Gyms & fitness studios** | Membership is ₹1,000–3,000/month, pure catchment play, and a person who just ate is the exact person thinking about the gym | `gym in Adajan Surat` |
| **Dental clinics** | High ticket (₹5,000–50,000), zero loyalty, chooses by proximity | `dentist in Vesu Surat` |
| **Salons & spas** | Repeat monthly, couples out for dinner are the buyer | `salon in Piplod Surat` |
| **Diagnostic labs / polyclinics** | Full-body checkup packages, runs on local trust | `pathology lab in Varachha Surat` |
| **Driving schools & coaching classes** | Admission season, parents eating out with kids | `coaching classes in Katargam Surat` |

**Tier 2 — good money, needs one more visit.**

| Category | Why | Search |
|---|---|---|
| **Clothing & boutique stores** | Wedding and festival season spikes | `boutique in Ring Road Surat` |
| **Jewellers** | Huge ticket; wants brand presence, not clicks | `jewellers in Mahidharpura Surat` |
| **Opticals** | Buy-one-get-one offers travel well on a small screen | `optical store in Adajan Surat` |
| **Mobile & electronics shops** | EMI offers, exchange offers | `mobile shop in Varachha Surat` |
| **Interior designers / modular kitchen** | One lead pays for a year of sponsorship | `interior designer in Vesu Surat` |
| **Real estate — new projects** | Biggest budgets in Surat, slowest to decide | `new projects in Vesu Surat` |
| **Car & bike service centres** | Service reminder offers | `car service in Pal Surat` |
| **Travel agents / tour packages** | Vacation season, families eating out together | `travel agency in Adajan Surat` |
| **Event & wedding planners, photographers** | Wedding season Nov–Feb | `wedding photographer in Surat` |
| **Packers & movers** | Pure local, always advertising | `packers and movers in Surat` |

**Tier 3 — only once you have 25+ restaurants.** Needs scale before they
care: banks and NBFCs, insurance agents, pharma chains, furniture
showrooms, play schools, pet clinics.

### Never, under any circumstances

**No food.** No restaurant, café, bakery, sweet shop, ice-cream parlour,
tiffin service, cloud kitchen, caterer, Zomato, Swiggy, or anything a
hungry person could leave the table for. One competitor's banner on a
restaurant's own guest screen and you lose the restaurant, not the sponsor.
This rule is non-negotiable and it goes in the sponsor contract.

Also out: alcohol and tobacco, loan apps and anything "instant cash",
matrimonial and dating, political parties, astrologers and faith healers,
and any clinic advertising a cure.

## 3. Getting the list (20 minutes per area, once)

Same method as `SALES_SURAT.md` §3 — **phone numbers are deliberately not
in this file.** A number from an old directory page is how you call a shop
that shut in 2023.

1. Google Maps → the search string from the table above.
2. Open each result. Take **name, phone, area, rating + review count**, and
   whether the photos show a big board outside — a shop that spends on a
   board spends on advertising.
3. 4.0+ and 50+ reviews goes on the list.
4. **Also** open their Instagram from the Maps listing. A business posting
   twice a week already has a marketing budget and a person who handles it.
   Those convert first.
5. Straight into **HQ → Leads → Add a lead**, area and come-back date filled.

Start with the areas where your restaurants already are. A sponsor in
Adajan is worth nothing until there are Jamavat restaurants in Adajan.

## 4. The price list

Keep it to three lines. Never quote per impression — nobody in Surat buys
advertising that way.

| Pack | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Ek vistaar** (one area) | All Jamavat restaurants in one area, one slot | **₹3,000 / month** |
| **Be vistaar** (two areas) | Two areas | **₹5,000 / month** |
| **Aakhu Surat** | Every Jamavat restaurant in the city | **₹9,000 / month** |

- Three months upfront: **one month free.**
- First sponsor in any category in an area gets **category exclusivity** —
  only one gym per area. Say this out loud; it is what closes the deal and
  it costs you nothing.
- First two sponsors ever: **₹1,500 for the first month**, because you need
  a screenshot and a testimonial more than you need their money.

**Revenue share with the restaurant: 50/50.** Tell the restaurant owner
before you sell anything. ₹1,500 a month off his Jamavat bill turns him
from "why is there an ad on my guest's phone" into the man who introduces
you to the gym owner he plays cricket with. That introduction is worth more
than the whole list above.

## 5. What to say

**At the door (10 seconds):**
> "Hu Jamavat mathi. Ame Surat na restaurants ma QR menu chalaviye chhiye —
> guest potana phone par order aape. Jamtaa jamtaa je screen jue che, ena
> par tamaru advertisement mukiye. Be minute joi lo."

**The 30-second pitch — phone in hand, a real guest screen open:**
> Adajan na paanch restaurant ma amaru QR chale che. Mahine 9,000 guest
> potana phone par menu khole che, ane order aapya pachhi das-pandar minute
> aa screen par bese che — jamvaanu aave tyaa sudhi.
>
> Tamaru banner ahi dekhaay. Ene click kare to sidho tamaro WhatsApp khule.
>
> Mahine ₹3,000. Hoarding na ₹15,000 thaay che ane e koi jotu nathi. Ane
> ek vistaar ma gym ek j rahe — tamara pachhi biju koi gym nahi.

Then **hand them the phone** with a live guest screen on it, their
competitor's category empty. The screen sells. The sentence only buys you
the phone.

**Objections:**

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Ketla loko joshe?" | Show the numbers, not adjectives: "Aa paanch restaurant, aa mahine 9,000 scan. Dar mahine report moklu chhu — ketla joyu, ketla click thaya." |
| "Pehla ek mahino free aapo" | "Free nahi, pan pehla mahine ₹1,500. Na game to biju mahine band — koi contract nahi." Never free: a free sponsor never converts and never values the slot. |
| "Mara customer restaurant ma nathi aavta" | Wrong category, or wrong area. Don't argue — this one is a no. Leave. |
| "Facebook/Instagram ads karu chhu" | "E barabar chhe, aa eni jagya nathi letu. Tya scroll ma ek second male che. Ahi guest das minute aaj screen par bese che, ane e tamara j vistaar no che." |
| "Restaurant wala ne vaandho nahi aave?" | "Restaurant ne enu bhaag male che, etle e potej kahe che. Ane food ni koi jaherat ame letaa j nathi — aa niyam chhe." |

**Never promise:** a number of clicks, a number of customers, sales, or
first position. Promise impressions and a monthly report — those you can
actually deliver. One promise you cannot keep costs you every other sponsor
in that street, because they all know each other.

how the number gets blocked and your business number gets reported.

## 8. Before you sell the first slot

Two things must exist, or you are selling something you cannot deliver:

1. **The ad slot has to be live** on the guest screen — image, link, and an
   impression/click counter. Nothing is built yet (see the ads plan).
2. **At least five restaurants in one area**, and all five owners told about
   the 50/50 share and having said yes. One area with five is sellable;
   fifteen restaurants scattered across Surat is not.

Until both are true, use this list for one thing only: walk into ten of
them, show the screen, and ask what they pay for a hoarding today. Ten
answers tell you whether ₹3,000 is too high or far too low — and that is
worth more than a guess.
